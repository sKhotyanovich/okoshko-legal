Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our application (the “App”) handles information. The App is a personal work organizer for beauty and service professionals: it keeps a work schedule, appointments, a client list, prices, income and expenses.

The short version: we, the developer, do not collect, receive or store any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. The App has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Who we are

The App is developed and published by Giles Vance (an individual developer), referred to as “we” or “us” in this Policy. Contact details are at the end of this Policy.

3. Information you enter in the App

To do its job, the App stores the information you type into it:

  • Client details — names, phone numbers, Instagram usernames and notes you add about your clients;
  • Appointments — dates, times, services, prices, discounts, payments and notes;
  • Your work data — your schedule, service list and prices, expenses and the statistics calculated from them;
  • Settings — preferences such as haptics and currency, stored on the device.

This information is stored locally in the App on your device. We have no access to it.

Because the App lets you store information about other people (your clients), you are responsible for having their permission or another lawful basis to keep their contact details and notes, as required by the law that applies to you.

4. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the App syncs your data across your own devices through Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) private database. This data belongs to your Apple Account: it is stored and protected by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy, and we as the developer cannot see or access it. You can turn off iCloud for the App at any time in your device’s Settings.

5. Backups you create

The App can create a backup file of your data. Where that file goes is always your choice:

  • Share sheet. You can save or send the backup file anywhere using the standard iOS share sheet (Files, a messenger, email and so on). The file is then handled by the service you choose.
  • Optional Telegram backup. If you set it up, the App sends the backup file and short service messages to a Telegram chat through a Telegram bot that you create yourself via Telegram’s @BotFather. You enter your own bot token and chat ID; the token is kept in the device’s Keychain. The backup is sent over HTTPS directly from your device to Telegram (api.telegram.org) — when you choose to send a backup now and automatically in the evening when your data has changed. When you connect the bot, it sends one test message to the chat. The file contains your client and appointment data, so it is stored by Telegram in your chat under Telegram’s Privacy Policy. We do not operate that bot and receive nothing from it. You can disconnect the bot at any time in the App, which erases the token and chat ID from the device.

6. Device permissions

  • Face ID / Touch ID / passcode — used only to lock the App so that only you can see your clients and appointments. Authentication is performed by iOS; the App never receives your biometric data.
  • Add to Photos — used only when you choose to save the image with your free slots to your photo library. The App can add images but does not read your photo library.

7. Information sent automatically

To show amounts in another currency, the App downloads public exchange rates from the National Bank of the Republic of Belarus (api.nbrb.by). The request contains no personal data; as with any internet request, the server receives your IP address as part of the network connection.

The App does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs, and it does not use the advertising identifier. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports under Apple’s own terms.

8. How information is used

Information you enter is used only to provide the App’s features on your devices: showing your schedule and free slots, keeping your client history, calculating statistics, syncing through your iCloud and creating backups you request. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

9. Third-party services

The App connects only to the following services, and only for the features described above:

  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit) — syncing your data across your devices;
  • Telegram — optional backups to your own bot, only if you set it up;
  • National Bank of the Republic of Belarus — public exchange rates.

When you tap a phone number, a messenger or an Instagram link, the App opens the corresponding app or website, which then handles your interaction under its own policy. We do not sell, rent or share personal data.

10. Security and data retention

Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud account for as long as you keep it in the App. Deleting a client or appointment removes it from the App and, through sync, from your other devices. Deleting the App removes its local data from the device; data in iCloud can be managed in your device’s iCloud settings. Backup files you have saved or sent elsewhere, including to Telegram, remain there until you delete them. Network requests made by the App use HTTPS.

11. International transfers

We do not transfer your data anywhere. Apple and Telegram may store data in their data centers in various countries according to their own policies.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the European Economic Area or the UK, or under US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data and to object to its processing. Since we hold no data about you, you exercise these rights directly in the App: you can view, edit and delete anything you entered and export it as a backup file. If you have a question, contact us using the details below.

13. Children’s privacy

The App is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

14. Third-party links

The App may open external apps and websites (such as Telegram, Instagram or messengers). We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices; please review their policies.
